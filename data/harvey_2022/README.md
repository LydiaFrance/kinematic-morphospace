# Harvey 2022 cadaver data

These two CSV files are a subset of the cadaver wing and body measurements
published by Harvey et al. (2022). They are redistributed here under the
terms of the original licence for reproducibility of the cross-species
analysis in this repository (see `examples/hawks/14_CrossSpeciesGeneralisation.ipynb`).

**Please cite the original paper and data deposit when using these files.**

## Files

| File | Description |
|------|-------------|
| `specimen_bodyinfo.csv` | Per-specimen body measurements (mass, body landmarks). |
| `specimen_winginfo.csv` | Per-specimen wing measurements (feather landmarks at maximum wingspan). |

## Citation

Harvey, C., Baliga, V. B., Wong, J. C. M., Altshuler, D. L., & Inman, D. J.
(2022). Birds can transition between stable and unstable states via wing
morphing. *Nature*, 603, 648–653. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-022-04477-8
